Compassionate, Evidence-Based Gynaecological & Reproductive Care

Dr. Geetha R is a distinguished Obstetrician, Gynaecologist, and Minimally Invasive Laparoscopic Surgeon renowned for balancing surgical excellence with heartfelt patient communication.
